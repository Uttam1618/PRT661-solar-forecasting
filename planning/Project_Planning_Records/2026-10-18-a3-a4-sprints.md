## Decisions

1. **Demand forecasting before weather forecast input.** The A3 decomposition showed perfect demand would capture 85% of the perfect foresight benefit against 52% for perfect solar, so A4 effort went to demand models rather than numerical weather prediction, which also had no available data source. Result: share of the perfect foresight benefit 39% to 70%.
2. **Solar caution 0.5 kept.** Chosen on 2024 data in A3 and confirmed on 2025.
3. **Monthly refit of the demand model.** Planning hour error 24.9 kW against 41.3 kW for a static model; the triggered refit is kept as a backup.
4. **Alert limits fixed on 2019 to 2020** and never refitted, so slow decline cannot hide in a moving baseline.
5. **Decision support, not control.** The dashboard is read only and the operator approves each overnight charge.
