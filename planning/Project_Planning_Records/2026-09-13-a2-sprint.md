## 1. Decision: change of primary dataset

**Decision.** The Master Meter 1 reference extract replaces the nineteen yearly extracts as the
project's primary dataset. The yearly extracts are retained as schema evidence and as the source
of site-level battery telemetry from 2024 onward.

**Reason.** Profiling before writing any ingestion code established that the nineteen yearly
extracts contain no meteorological columns at all: no irradiance, no temperature, no humidity.
A solar generation forecast cannot be built on them. Master Meter 1 carries those covariates at
the same five minute resolution, has a stable schema, and is the series on which the published
benchmark reports.

**Authority.** Approved by the Project Lead under the scope-change rule in
`Task_Allocation/task-allocation.md`. Recorded here before the ingestion work began.

**Consequence.** The A1 architecture is otherwise unchanged. The reconciliation stage becomes a
profiling and evidence stage rather than a merge across nineteen schemas.

## 2. Decisions taken during development

| Decision | Reason |
|---|---|
| Degradation fitted from 2014 only | Fitting from 2009 returns +0.748 kW per year because the site was still being commissioned. The stable-array window gives -0.454 kW per year, inside the published range for crystalline silicon. |
| Cleaning applied before aggregation | Aggregating first let sentinel values into hourly means, leaving an air temperature of -39.988 C in the store. |
| The cumulative register exempted from sentinel rules | It is a counter, not an instrument reading, and legitimately exceeds any instrument bound. |
| The target is never interpolated | Filling the variable being predicted manufactures agreement between model and data. |
| Models trained with absolute error loss where MAE is reported | Training on squared error and reporting MAE produced negative skill at 24 hours and an apparently unstable fold. |
| Benchmark comparison reported with parity conditions rather than as a headline | The published work solves a contemporaneous mapping; this project forecasts ahead. Claiming a win on R2 would not be defensible. |
| Physical bounds taken from instrument limits and the Alice Springs climate record | Percentile-based thresholds let a systematic fault define what counts as normal. |

## 3. Quality gate outcome

Eight gates were defined with quantitative thresholds. Seven pass. **One fails:** 24 hour
prediction interval daylight coverage is 0.706 against a required band of 0.75 to 0.85. Under the
stated rule the interval is published with an explicit warning rather than withdrawn, because the
point forecast is unaffected and an interval with a stated caveat is more useful to an operator
than none. Recalibration is an Assessment 3 task.
